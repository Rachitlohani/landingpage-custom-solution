  • Customs Brokerage OS: Operational workbench for licensed customs brokers with confidence-based exception queues and side-by-side evidence inspection.
  • Trade Advisory & Policy Agent: Governs USMCA rules of origin, Section 301/232 tariff exclusions, ADD/CVD screening, and binding CROSS rulings.

  • AI Classification Engine: Classifies products into 10-digit HTSUS codes with legal General Rules of Interpretation (GRI 1-6) grounding.

Core Research & Whitepapers

Building an AI-Native Trade Knowledge Graph: Why Document AI Isn't Enough

Today's trade compliance systems think in terms of documents. Qubere thinks in terms of knowledge. Raw shipping uploads create an ephemeral transaction graph containing connected Supplier, Product, HTS Code, and Port nodes. Once filed, verified durable entities are promoted to the permanent Enterprise Knowledge Graph while transient document noise is pruned.

Why Customs AI Must Be Evidence-Backed Before It Is Autonomous

General-purpose LLMs predict likely tokens, but in customs compliance, ungrounded AI leads to costly misclassifications and CBP penalties. Qubere decouples decision proposals from legal verification by grounding every proposal in bounding-box document provenance and binding CROSS ruling citations.
